## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Upload Instructions for Chrome Developer Console

1. Log into your [Chrome Developer Dashboard](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/devconsole).
2. Select your extension (**Rewriter AI**).
3. Navigate to **Store Listing** in the left menu.
4. Update the **Description** and **Short Description** using the text provided above.
5. Under **Graphic Assets**:
   - Upload the 5 screenshots from `chrome-store-assets/screenshots/`.
   - Upload `small-promo-440x280.png` under **Small promo tile (440x280)**.
   - Upload `marquee-promo-1400x560.png` under **Marquee promo tile (1400x560)**.
6. Click **Save draft** and then **Submit for review**!
