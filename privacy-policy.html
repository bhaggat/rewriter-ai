Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-07-31 · Version 1.0.0

Rewriter AI lets you chat with, and rewrite text using, an AI provider of your choice — OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Groq, Mistral, or OpenRouter — using an API key you supply. This page explains exactly what the extension does with your data.

At a glance

No Does Rewriter AI have a backend server?No. It's a pure client — there is nothing to send your data to, and no server to breach.
No Do the developers ever see your text or API keys?No. Both stay on your device, or travel directly from your browser to the AI provider you configured.
No Is any data sold or shared with advertisers?No. There is no analytics, tracking, or advertising code in this extension.
Local Where are API keys and chat history stored?In your browser's local extension storage, on your device only. They are never synced to a Google account or any server.

01What the extension does

Rewriter AI adds two features to your browser:

  • Inline rewrite. When you focus a text field on a webpage, a small icon appears. Clicking it lets you rewrite the field's text using a preset or custom instruction.
  • Chat popup. The extension's toolbar icon opens a chat window where you can converse directly with your chosen AI provider.

Both features send text to the AI provider you've configured, using an API key you provide, and display the response back to you. That's the extent of what the extension does.

03Where your data goes

When you rewrite text or send a chat message, the text and your API key for that provider are sent directly from your browser to the provider's own API. Rewriter AI does not relay, log, or store this traffic anywhere else. Each provider's own privacy policy governs how they handle that request:

Rewriter AI only contacts the provider(s) you've personally added an API key for. It never contacts a provider you haven't configured.

04What we don't do

  • We don't operate any server. There is no backend to send your data to.
  • We don't collect analytics, telemetry, or crash reports.
  • We don't use cookies or any cross-site tracking.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • We don't read or transmit the content of any webpage other than the specific text field you're actively editing, and only while the inline-rewrite feature is on.

05Your control over your data

Everything Rewriter AI stores lives in your browser's local extension storage:

  • Delete it any time by removing the extension, or by clearing individual API keys, presets, or chat history from the Settings page.
  • Turn off inline rewrite globally, or exclude specific sites, from the Settings page.
  • Nothing leaves your device unless you actively trigger a rewrite or chat message.

06Children's privacy

Rewriter AI is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
