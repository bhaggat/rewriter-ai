Privacy Policy

Effective: August 1, 2026 • Version 1.0.0

Rewriter AI is engineered with a strict zero-trust, privacy-first model. You bring your own API keys for OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Groq, Mistral, or OpenRouter. Your text and keys travel directly from your browser to your configured AI provider—with zero intermediate servers in between.

Privacy Architecture at a Glance
NO SERVER
Does Rewriter AI operate a backend server?

No. Rewriter AI is 100% client-side software running inside your Chrome browser.

NO LOGGING
Do developers ever see your keys or text?

No. Everything stays in your local browser storage (chrome.storage.local) or is sent encrypted via HTTPS directly to your selected AI provider.

NO TRACKING
Is there any analytics, telemetry, or data selling?

No. There are zero tracking scripts, zero advertising SDKs, and zero telemetry calls.

01 Extension Core Functionality

Rewriter AI provides two key user-facing capabilities:

  • Inline Text Rewriter: When you focus an editable text element on a webpage and click the icon or press Alt+Shift+R, the extension sends the selected text to your configured AI model and replaces it upon your request.
  • Toolbar Chat Window: Clicking the extension action icon opens a conversational interface to interact directly with your selected AI model.

02 Browser Permissions & Data Usage

PermissionPurpose
storage Saves your API keys, model preferences, prompt presets, and chat history locally on your device using chrome.storage.local.
activeTab Allows the keyboard shortcut and toolbar popup to inspect the text field you are actively focused on.
Host Permissions Allows direct HTTPS API connections from your browser to provider endpoints (api.openai.com, generativelanguage.googleapis.com, api.anthropic.com, api.x.ai, api.groq.com, api.mistral.ai, openrouter.ai).
Content Scripts Detects when you focus an editable input or textarea element on a webpage to show the rewrite floating widget. It only reads the text inside the active element when triggered—never scanning the background page.

04 Your Control & Data Deletion

You maintain 100% control over all data stored by Rewriter AI:

  • Clear Local Storage: You can delete any or all API keys, chat history, or custom presets at any time from the Settings page.
  • Disable Inline Rewriter: Turn off the floating inline trigger globally or exclude specific website domains in the Options page.
  • Complete Removal: Uninstalling the extension automatically removes all stored keys, preferences, and chat history from your browser.
